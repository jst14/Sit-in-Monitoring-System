About The System

Our Purpose

The purpose of the CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is to simplify the administration of student sit-in sessions in the labs of the College of Computer Studies. It ensures transparent and effective laboratory utilization monitoring by replacing manual logbook tracking with a quick, digital solution that benefits administrators and students alike.

Laboratory Coverage

The system covers six CCS computer laboratories — Lab 524, Lab 525, Lab 528, Lab 530, Lab 542 and Lab MAC. Each laboratory has 40 PCs that students can view and reserve in real time through the student dashboard.

For Students

Students can reserve a computer, keep track of their sit-in history, see the leaderboard, check the number of sessions left (30 each semester), and get points for finishing sessions. After a single registration, all functions are available, making laboratory reservations easy.

For Administrators

From a safe, feature-rich admin interface, CCS lab administrators can log students in and out, see real-time sit-in activity, manage student data, create reports, publish announcements, award points, and track feedback.
