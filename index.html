College of Computer Studies: Sit-in Monitoring System

Welcome to the CCS Sit-in Monitoring System of the University of Cebu - Main Campus!

This system is designed to provide students with a convenient and efficient way to manage laboratory sit-in sessions, monitor computer availability, and stay updated with laboratory announcements.

Located at the 5th Floor of the CCS Department, the system helps students easily access laboratory services anytime through its 24/7 online platform.

Key Features

Reservation and Sit-in Recording

Students can request and reserve laboratory sit-in sessions online. Administrators will review and record each student's sit-in time to ensure proper monitoring and management of laboratory usage.

Laboratory Announcements

Stay informed with the latest announcements regarding laboratory schedules, temporary closures, maintenance updates, and unavailable or broken computers. This helps students plan their laboratory sessions more efficiently.

Real-Time Laboratory and PC Availability

The system allows users to view available laboratories and monitor open computer slots in real time. Each laboratory contains up to 40 computer slots, making it easier for students to find available workstations.

24/7 Online Accessibility

The Sit-in Monitoring System is accessible anytime and anywhere, providing students with continuous access to reservations, schedules, and laboratory information.

Session Monitoring Per Semester

Each student is provided with 30 sit-in sessions per semester. The system automatically tracks and monitors the remaining sessions to ensure fair and organized laboratory usage for all CCS students.

Purpose of the System

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System aims to improve laboratory management, enhance student convenience, and provide an organized way of monitoring computer laboratory usage within the College of Computer Studies.

About The System

Our Purpose

The purpose of the CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is to simplify the administration of student sit-in sessions in the labs of the College of Computer Studies. It ensures transparent and effective laboratory utilization monitoring by replacing manual logbook tracking with a quick, digital solution that benefits administrators and students alike.

Laboratory Coverage

The system covers six CCS computer laboratories — Lab 524, Lab 525, Lab 528, Lab 530, Lab 542 and Lab MAC. Each laboratory has 40 PCs that students can view and reserve in real time through the student dashboard.

For Students

Students can reserve a computer, keep track of their sit-in history, see the leaderboard, check the number of sessions left (30 each semester), and get points for finishing sessions. After a single registration, all functions are available, making laboratory reservations easy.

For Administrators

From a safe, feature-rich admin interface, CCS lab administrators can log students in and out, see real-time sit-in activity, manage student data, create reports, publish announcements, award points, and track feedback.
